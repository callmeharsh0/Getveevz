GetVeevz — Website Brief
Purpose: Build a high-converting website that makes the GetVeevz offer immediately clear and drives qualified
prospects toward booking a call / starting a campaign.
1. Main Goal
 We turn existing long-form content into large-scale short-form distribution.
The website should make prospects understand what we do within 5 seconds and drive them toward booking a call /
starting a campaign.
2. Target Customer
Founders  Entrepreneurs  Creators / personal brands  Businesses with existing long-form content  Podcasts /
content-led brands
The ideal customer already creates content but wants more reach and distribution without having to build and
manage the entire clipping operation themselves.
3. Core Positioning
Main message
 Turn Your Long-Form Content Into a Short-Form Distribution Engine
GetVeevz takes existing long-form content and turns it into coordinated short-form distribution across Instagram,
YouTube Shorts and TikTok. We handle the clipping operation, campaign coordination, distribution and tracking.
Important
Do not position us as simply a video-editing agency. The website should communicate: Content → Clipping →
Distribution → Tracking.

5. Design Direction
Feel: Premium + Modern + Tech-enabled + Aggressive + Results-focused
Use a dark / premium visual system, large typography, strong animations, social media content integrated into the
design, real campaign screenshots, minimal paragraphs, lots of visual proof and clear CTAs throughout.
Avoid: Generic agency templates, corporate-looking layouts, stock photos, huge blocks of text, overexplaining the
service, too many navigation options and generic headings like “About Us” and “Our Services”.
The visitor should be able to scan the entire page and understand the business without reading every paragraph.
